### Key Differences

- **Antaios targets importers/operators** — full EUDR due diligence under Article 10(2), not just trader-level traceability. If you import regulated commodities into the EU, you need operator-level compliance.
- **EUDRReady targets downstream traders** — traders who purchase from EU-based operators. It does not cover the full operator due diligence workflow.
- **Per-plot pricing adds up fast** — with 500+ smallholder suppliers (common in coffee and cocoa), Plotvera's €99/mo only covers 500 plots. Antaios gives you unlimited from day one.
- **No hidden costs** — no per-shipment fees, no per-plot overage charges, no enterprise sales process required. Self-serve onboarding in an afternoon.

## Why Unlimited Matters

Most EUDR compliance tools charge per plot or per shipment. For importers with hundreds of smallholder suppliers across multiple commodities, this creates unpredictable costs and forces you to limit which shipments you process. Antaios gives you unlimited capacity so every shipment gets full compliance treatment.

Psychology Power-Ups Used (from research)

🧠 Prospection
User sees "Pro You" future self. Personalized projections based on their actual usage. WOOP framework: Wish → Outcome → Obstacle → Plan.
⚖️ Mental Contrasting
Current You vs Pro You comparison. Positive future first, then current limits. The tension creates motivation.
📉 Loss Aversion
"Without Pro, your scans stop." "Cost of non-compliance: €19,200/yr." Losses feel 2x more painful than gains feel good.
🏠 Endowment Effect
"You've completed 14 assessments" — user already owns this work. Upgrading protects their investment.
🔄 Zeigarnik Effect
"60% complete — finish with Pro." Incomplete tasks create cognitive tension. The brain wants closure.
⚓ Pricing Anchoring
3-tier display (Free → Direct → Enterprise). Enterprise at Custom anchors Direct at €500 as reasonable. Annual default with "2 months free" framing.
👥 Social Proof
Testimonials with name + role + metric. Specific recognizable logos. Authority bias: EUDR regulation as trust signal.
🛡️ Risk Reversal
30-day money-back guarantee. "Cancel anytime." No credit card required for trial. Reduces purchase anxiety at €500/mo.
